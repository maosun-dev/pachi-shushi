Hello, thank you for reviewing our app. Here is the requested information.

1. Screen recording
Attached is a screen recording on a physical iPhone running the latest iOS. It starts from launching the app and shows the typical flow: choosing an item, entering the amounts, recording, picking a store / machine / tag, viewing the yearly, monthly, store, machine and tag summaries, adding a memo and a photo, and exporting CSV.
The app has no account registration or login, no user-generated content shared with other users, and no paid content or in-app purchases.

2. Purpose and target audience
"ワンタップ収支" is a personal notebook for adults in Japan who play pachinko and pachislot as a hobby. After playing, the user enters how much they spent and how much they got back, and the app keeps the history and shows totals and win rates by month, store, machine and user-made tags. It helps users see their real balance and keep their spending under control.
The app does not provide any gambling. It has no games, no betting, no real-money or virtual-currency transactions, no prizes, and no links to gambling or pachinko hall services. It only records numbers that the user types in.

3. How to use the main features
No login or sample files are needed.
- On the main screen, tap an item (for example "1パチ" or "20スロ"), enter "投資" (spent) and "回収" (got back), and tap the record button.
- The "店 / 機種 / タグ" buttons above the amounts let the user choose or add a store name, machine name and tags.
- Tap the yearly balance at the top to open the details: monthly / store / machine / tag summaries, and each record where a memo, photos and videos can be added.
- CSV export and import are at the bottom of the details screen.

4. External services
- Google AdMob (banner and interstitial ads). This is the only external service.
All records, photos and videos are stored only on the device. The app has no server, no authentication service, no payment processor and no AI service.

5. Regional differences
The app works the same in all regions. Its interface is in Japanese.

6. Regulated industry / third-party material
The app is a personal record-keeping tool and does not operate gambling or any regulated service. It contains no third-party protected material; store and machine names are typed in freely by the user.

Thank you.
